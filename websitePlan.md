# 2. Technology Stack & System Architecture

## Overview

BeastBuck is powered by a modern cloud-first architecture that is designed to be:

- Fast
- Secure
- Responsive
- Scalable
- Reliable
- Easy to maintain

The architecture follows one important rule:

**Deliver the best possible experience while using 100% free services during development.**

Every technology is chosen because it is reliable, integrates well with the ecosystem, and allows BeastBuck to continue growing in the future without requiring major architectural changes.

The platform should always remain lightweight, efficient, and accessible while being ready for future upgrades whenever needed.
# Frontend Technologies

The frontend is responsible for everything users see, touch, and interact with inside BeastBuck.

Every technology has been carefully selected to make the platform feel fast, beautiful, responsive, and enjoyable while remaining completely free to develop and maintain.

Our goal is to create an experience that feels more like a modern mobile app than a traditional website.

# 3. User System, Membership & Role Architecture

## Overview

BeastBuck is a private, community-driven digital ecosystem designed to maintain a safe, high-quality, and collaborative environment.

Creating an account **does not** automatically grant full access to the platform.

Every person begins as a **Registered User**. They can explore selected public areas, experience BeastBuck, and understand the community before deciding whether to become a Member.

To unlock collaboration, creation tools, research, publishing, leadership opportunities, and advanced features, a user must submit a **Membership Application**. Every application is reviewed by the leadership team before approval.

This approval process helps maintain quality, trust, safety, and a positive community culture.

The platform always follows the principle:

> **Explore first. Contribute after approval. Lead through trust.**

---

# User Journey

Every account follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Visitor
      │
      ▼
Sign Up / Sign In
      │
      ▼
Registered User
      │
      ▼
Apply for Membership
      │
      ▼
CEO / Co-CEO Review
      │
      ▼
Approved Member
      │
      ▼
Experienced Contributor
      │
      ▼
Community Leader
      │
      ▼
Co-CEO
      │
      ▼
CEO
```

No shortcuts exist.

Every role is earned through trust and contribution.

---

# Core Principles

The entire permission system follows these principles.

- Nobody becomes a Member automatically.
- Registration is different from Membership.
- Every permission is role-based.
- Pages only display actions users are allowed to perform.
- Hidden actions are better than disabled actions.
- Public users should enjoy exploring BeastBuck without feeling restricted.
- Members unlock creation and collaboration.
- Leadership roles manage the ecosystem, not ordinary Members.
- Security is enforced both in the frontend and Firebase Security Rules.
- Client permissions improve UX, while backend rules guarantee security.

- Upload
- Publish

- Delete

- Manage
- Invite

Where appropriate, display informative cards such as:

Include a clear **Apply for Membership** button wherever it makes sense.

---

## Portfolio

Registered Users do **not** have a BeastBuck Portfolio.

The Portfolio is created automatically **only after Membership is approved**.

Before approval:

- Portfolio route should not exist.
- Profile displays only basic account information.
- Achievement statistics remain hidden.
- Public contribution timeline does not exist.

---

## Backend Permissions

Frontend visibility alone is **not** sufficient.

Firebase Security Rules must enforce these restrictions.

Only Approved Members and higher roles may:

- Write protected Firestore collections
- Upload media
- Publish content
- Create documents
- Edit collaborative resources
- Delete ecosystem content

Registered Users may only write to:

- Their own profile
- Settings
- Membership application
- Personal preferences
- Notification preferences

Everything else must be read-only or completely inaccessible.

It is much more than a place to chat with AI.

It is a complete AI Creation Playground where members can learn AI, master Prompt Engineering, build powerful AI assistants, customize personalities, upload knowledge, collaborate with other creators, publish creations, participate in competitions, and become recognized AI Innovators.

The entire experience should make AI feel exciting, creative, approachable, and rewarding for beginners while still providing powerful tools for advanced creators.

The interface should feel like a blend of ChatGPT GPTs, Google AI Studio, Character AI, Claude Projects, Canva, Figma, and Duolingo—combined into one colorful, highly interactive, animation-rich ecosystem designed for curious minds.

Every interaction should inspire members to think:

> **"I can create something amazing with AI."**

AI Studio should never feel like a technical dashboard.

It should feel like a magical workshop where ideas come to life.

---

# Vision

AI should empower creativity rather than replace it.

Members should be able to

- Learn AI fundamentals
- Master Prompt Engineering
- Build AI Assistants
- Create AI Characters
- Upload Knowledge
- Improve Existing AI
- Collaborate with Friends
- Share AI with the Community
- Participate in AI Challenges
- Become AI Creators
- Become Prompt Engineers
- Explore Future AI Technologies

without writing complex code.

Every member should feel capable of building useful AI regardless of previous experience.
